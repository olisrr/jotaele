In Santa Isabel Square since 1981

Jota Ele opened in 1981 and soon became the social and gastronomic reference point of the moment, thanks to its avant-garde architecture, its way of interpreting cuisine and its location in the central Santa Isabel Square.

In 1986 Francisco González, who had been part of the team from the beginning, took the lead of the business and created his own new style of cooking.

Jota Ele, the constant renewal of a classic

Jota Ele is a team recognised for its professional service, which creates a pleasant, family atmosphere.

An elegant and welcoming space with a great bar where you can taste the greatness and authenticity of its cooking.

Menu

Today, Jota Ele is a restaurant built on the quality of the product, a cuisine that seeks a balance between tradition and evolution. Honest, quality cooking that champions local produce, bought daily at our markets.

See the full menu

Wines

Each wine on our list is the result of a tradition that has marked a path and a style, and of the drive to discover small winegrowers who take care of every detail and make each wine unique with their way of understanding life. Francisco González, a wine lover and founding member of the Association of Sommeliers of the Region of Murcia, gathers in the restaurant’s cellar many references that spark his interest and passion for oenology. Wines that enhance the flavour of a very personal cuisine and invite everyone to enjoy them together.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Jota Ele restaurant?

At Plaza de Santa Isabel 6, in the centre of Murcia, Spain (30004). We have been here since 1981.

How do I book a table?

On WhatsApp at +34 648 25 19 74, online at jotaelerestaurante.com/reservas or by calling +34 968 220 730.

What kind of food do you serve?

Product-led cooking between tradition and evolution, with local produce bought daily at Murcia’s markets, paired with a cellar of small-grower wines.

Do you have Christmas menus?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp and we will send you the available Christmas menus. You can also see them at jotaelerestaurante.com/navidad or call +34 968 220 730.

Do you offer take-away?

Yes, through our “En casa” service. Order by calling +34 968 220 730 or emailing franjotaele@gmail.com.

Can I give a meal at Jota Ele as a gift?

Yes. The Jota Ele experience is a menu for two for €50 (VAT included), valid for 12 months from purchase.
